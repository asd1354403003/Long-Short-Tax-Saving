Grouping, storage and timing

Universe: russell1000_iwb_proxy; grouping: sector. Membership and sector labels come from the same historical snapshot. Eligibility is recomputed at each decision for the factors required by that submodel; groups with fewer than ten eligible stocks do not rank. The source's Other label is retained.

Active database: aqr_research/data/aqr.sqlite3, relative to the project root. Original compressed CSVs, download URLs, hashes, retrieval dates and source-to-local ticker mappings are preserved. This page reads embedded cached data and makes no automatic source requests.

Month-end information includes prices and financials already available under the recorded date policy. A changing monthly holdings list does not mean the index selects a new top 1,000 by market capitalization every month.

Source: iShares IWB. Default routing: AQR settings; the selected monthly preset overrides baseline execution fields.
